# Steam Controller (2026)
- **Steam Controller as a controller type.** **Controller type**, for the host and for each device, now offers **Steam Controller**: games and Steam see a wired 2026 Steam Controller with both trackpads, back grips, grip and stick touch, motion and a battery. A client with one touchpad has it split into the left and right pads. A client that has a real Steam Controller and announces it also gets Steam's pad haptics and settings, and its firmware details, so Steam doesn't offer a firmware update for the virtual one; any other client gets rumble made from those haptics. Nothing changes unless you pick it. Don't start a controller firmware update from Steam while streaming: it would go to the virtual controller.
- **Updated virtual gamepad driver.** Setup installs libvirtualgamepad 0.1.0-beta.10 (driver 0.1.0.92, signed as in Vibepollo 2.0.6) over 0.1.0-beta.6. For existing pads: a virtual DualSense no longer shows up twice in Steam, as DualSense, DualShock 4 and Switch Pro now carry their real hardware IDs; the Switch Pro's gyro turns at the right speed (it ran about 5.5 times too fast), on SDL's axes, with rumble and battery levels read as on the real pad; DualShock 4 and DualSense motion timing follows the driver's clock, battery levels no longer read up to 10% high, and two fingers no longer merge into one touch; and pads survive sleep and resume more reliably. Xbox pads are unchanged. Run setup with the gamepad driver selected to update it.
