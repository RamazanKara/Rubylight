- Dual screen mode: a device with a second screen streams the same game on it too (needs Rubylight 3.11.0).
- Faster frames: PyroWave and the new engine hand frames to the decoder without extra copies, and the Vulkan renderer waits for the decoder on the GPU.
- A decoder that falls behind no longer shows as network loss or lowers the bitrate; it gets its own Decoder drops line.
- Stream menu, dialogs, keyboards and stats wear the same glass and read over bright games.
