## en
- Nightjar for Windows and Linux has a **Help** page: getting started, every keyboard shortcut, the controller's buttons drawn in your controller's layout, sharing, and fixes for common problems. **F1** or **?** opens it from any page.
- In a stream, **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H** or **Shortcuts & help** in the stream controls lists the stream's shortcuts. The stream controls are regrouped with icons, show each action's shortcut, dim the game behind them and set Disconnect apart.
