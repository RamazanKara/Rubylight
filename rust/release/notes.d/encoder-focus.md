- **Encoder focus (Experimental, off by default).** `encoder_focus = static` gives still parts of the picture, such as a HUD, text or an editor, more of the bitrate than moving ones, through AMF's ROI importance map, and `centre` also favours the middle of the screen. The host checks at startup that the encoder honours the map for each codec; a codec that fails the check streams exactly as before, with focus reported unsupported. The map comes from the converter with no added wait.
- Focus leaves the 30 frames after a keyframe without a map, while the rate control settles: maps there made frames up to 145 KB against an 83 KB cap at HEVC 1080p60 20 Mbps. A scene cut on a frame with a map is still about twice its size without focus.
