## en
- Play together from your own devices: on a PC with guests turned on (When another device joins the running game, in the host's Play together settings), Join on a game another device plays asks that device to let you in, with your own controller and the keyboard and mouse only if the player grants them.
- The device playing is asked who wants to join, with Let in and Deny, and Guests in the stream menu changes what each guest may do, hands over the keyboard and mouse, and kicks or blocks a guest.

## desktop
- Join on a game another device plays asks that device to let you in; the device playing sees a notice and answers under Guests in the stream menu's More page.
