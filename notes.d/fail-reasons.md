## en
- When the PC ends a stream on an error, Nightjar asks the PC why and shows its answer in the error dialog (for example that HDR is on but the display is not in HDR), on Android and on Windows and Linux. PCs that don't say keep the error as before.
- A stream the PC closes before its first picture no longer tells you to close protected videos or restart the PC: the game has usually just closed, so the message says to start it again.
