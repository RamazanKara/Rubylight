## en
- Hold Guide to open the stream menu: holding a controller's Guide (or Back, with Back button as Guide) for 1 second opens the stream menu; a short press still goes to the PC as Guide. Off, 0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2 seconds in Settings.
- The classic on-screen controls get an opacity per control: while resizing, touch a control and set its opacity beside its size.
- In the stream menu's bitrate dialogs, D-pad Up and Down move between the slider and the fields at once.
