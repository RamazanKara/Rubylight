## en
- The modern on-screen controller can add a WASD stick in Edit controller layout: it looks and moves like the other sticks but holds W, A, S and D, both keys on a diagonal, and lets every key go when you lift.
- The WASD stick vibrates once when it enters W, A, S or D and with a firmer click when it enters a diagonal, only on a change.
- Taps on the trackpads and their mouse buttons now vibrate too. Vibrate on press in Settings → Controls covers the modern controller, the WASD stick, the PC keyboard and the trackpads.
