## en
- Two new stream presets: Smooth TV keeps your screen's resolution and refresh rate and paces frames for the smoothest motion, and Wi-Fi / Away streams at two thirds of your screen's resolution at 60 fps for a weaker network. The four presets stand as two rows of two on a phone and in one row on a wider screen, a long name wraps inside its chip instead of being cut, and Custom stands out when no preset matches.

## desktop
- On desktop, the preset chips stay inside their row in a small window or at a large interface size: one line under the label, then two lines of two.
