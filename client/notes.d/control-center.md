## en
- New in a stream: the Control Center replaces the stream menu, and settings, help and hints call it that in every language. A rail of modules (Quick, Picture, Audio, Clips, PC, Controller, Guests, Power) leads to each one's cards, with Return to stream, Disconnect and Quit game under every page. It docks at the side of the screen, or rises as a sheet on a phone held upright; a controller's triggers step through the modules.

## desktop
- On desktop, the Control Center docks at the right edge, Ctrl+Tab steps through its modules as the triggers do, and it opens where you left it. Session and graphics, and Shortcuts & help, open inside it like its other pages instead of in a separate panel.
- The Control Center and the stream's overlays keep the night look over the game in the day and OLED themes. Quick keeps what the PC said about how the stream started after its toast is gone, and Picture shows the PC's video encoder load while the PC reports it.
- While recording, the Control Center's Stop recording card shows a stop mark instead of a play mark, and a long card label such as Session and graphics takes a second line instead of being cut.
