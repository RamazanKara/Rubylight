- **Control messages arrive right after a network outage.** When the network came back after an outage shorter than `ping_timeout` (10 s by default), what the host had sent in the meantime (an end of session, rumble, HDR metadata changes, link notices) could take another 1 to 3 s to reach the device, and in a simulation the host dropped a device that had been back for seconds after 3 of 4 outages. The host now resends these messages as soon as it hears from the device again: 85 ms after a 10 s outage with `ping_timeout = 15000` on loopback, against 1.0 to 2.9 s before. Every Moonlight client benefits with no client changes. In steady 5 % loss the host resends exactly as before; when only the device's datagrams get through, it adds a few resends once, not one round per gap.
