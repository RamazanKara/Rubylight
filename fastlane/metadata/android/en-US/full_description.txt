Stream your PC with low latency. Rubylight connects to Rubylight hosts and other stream hosts.

Bring your desktop and apps to your Android phone, tablet or TV. Pair your PC, open your library and start a stream. Use touch controls, a controller, or a keyboard and mouse.

Choose Native for your screen's resolution and maximum refresh rate, or Battery saver for lower-power streaming. Save separate settings for each PC. Check latency and frame rate in the Statistics or Debug overlay. Open the stream menu to adjust controls and settings while you stream.

Rubylight is free and open source. No ads or in-app purchases.
