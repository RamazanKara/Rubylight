## en
- Home has a View button: show games as a grid or in rows, grouped by store or as one library, at a small, medium or large card size. Phones, tablets and TVs keep their usual look until you change it.
- On a TV, the grid scrolls only up and down.
- A game or app name too long for its tile scrolls along its line while the tile has focus, so the artwork stays visible.
- Desktop: the same View options on Home, and a long game name scrolls under its focused cover.
