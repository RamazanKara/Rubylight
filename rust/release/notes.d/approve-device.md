# Pairing from the couch
- **Approve a new device from a paired one.** A device with the new **Approve new devices** permission sees who waits to pair and enters the PIN the new device shows, so a TV or console no longer means a walk to the PC. The new device gets Play, never more than the approver holds. Three wrong PINs end an address's requests for five minutes, a wrong PIN holds that address for 30 seconds, and every approval raises a toast, a log line and the `pairing.paired` webhook, naming both devices. The first device paired on a host from now on has the permission, later devices get it under Devices, Other, and devices already paired are unchanged by the upgrade. It allows nothing else, and an approved device doesn't inherit it. Host control approves too.
- The pairing toast now opens the Devices page with the PIN field focused, also after signing in, and the tray has a new **Pair a device** item that opens the page at the QR code.
