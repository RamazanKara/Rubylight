## en
- Settings → Controls → Gyroscope mapping fixes motion aiming that turns the wrong way and sets its speed, per controller: a Flydigi Apex 4 and Vader 4 Pro preset for Switch mode, or your own profiles with axis choice, inversion and left-right and up-down sensitivity.
- Show motion readings, under Diagnostics, shows the motion each controller sends during a stream and records it to a CSV file.

## desktop
- Each controller's Controller buttons page sets its gyroscope axes, inversion and left-right and up-down sensitivity under Motion.
