- **Keep monitors off while paused now lasts as long as the display is kept.** With an exclusive layout, the monitors came back on a few seconds after a device disconnected, whatever **Keep a disconnected display for** said (issue #92). With **Keep monitors off while paused** on and **Restore displays on disconnect** off, they now stay off for that time, or until the app closes. The restore hotkey still brings them back at any time.
