# Keep your other host
- **Try Rubylight while Vibepollo, Apollo or Sunshine stays installed.** Setup now asks when it finds another host: replace it, which still imports its settings and uninstalls it and stays the default, or keep it and install alongside (`--side-by-side` for a quiet install). Alongside, setup leaves that host, its drivers and its profile alone. Rubylight gets its own service (`RubylightService`), its own ports (base 48589, or the next free range) and new settings, so devices pair again; add the PC to Moonlight or Nightjar with the address setup shows, such as `192.168.1.20:48589`. Updates, repairs and uninstalling keep to that.
