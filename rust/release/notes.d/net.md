# Network
- **Error correction a device chooses.** A device that supports it can pick its own share of recovery packets for the video, or let the host follow its once-a-second loss reports: the host keeps the last ten seconds and sends the smallest share that keeps a lost frame under 0.1 %. **Most error correction a device may ask for** (`fec_max_percentage`, 50 % by default) caps both. For such a device, recovery packets for H.264, HEVC and AV1 come out of the video bitrate instead of on top of it, and the split follows the loss reports as they arrive (at once when loss rises, after a clear drop when it falls), without an extra keyframe. Other Moonlight clients keep **Error correction** and **Adapt error correction** as before.
- **Bandwidth test.** Paired devices that support it can time a short download (64 KB to 4 MB, at most 3 seconds) before they pick a starting bitrate, for every codec. The host refuses it while a stream runs or starts and limits how often a device may test. `bandwidth_probe` (on by default) turns it off.
- **Each launch starts one stream.** A launch now starts exactly one stream: a second start with the same launch is refused, an RTSP handshake that stalls is closed after 10 seconds, and a launch nobody connects to expires after 30 seconds (a minute at most while its handshake runs) with a line in the log.
