# Nucleus Co-op background streams
- **Your controller reaches the background game again.** A background stream started soon after another one now unplugs the earlier session's virtual controllers first, so the game reads your controller instead of a leftover one. When one of the PC's own controllers sits ahead of yours, the stream says so.
- **The PC's keyboard and mouse stay out of a background game.** Keys, clicks, the wheel and cursor positions from the PC no longer reach it, even when the game comes to the front.
- **Background games stay off your monitors.** Unreal Engine and Unity games start in a window, so they stay where Rubylight parks them instead of going fullscreen on the PC's monitor. A game that keeps coming back to a monitor, or minimises itself when it loses the focus, is put back for the whole stream, not just the first few seconds.
