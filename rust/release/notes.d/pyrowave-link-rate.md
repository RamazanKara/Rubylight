# PyroWave on a slow Wi-Fi link
- **PyroWave comes down to what Wi-Fi carries.** The host already lowered the bitrate of H.264, HEVC and AV1 streams when the network lost more than error correction could repair; PyroWave streams were left out. A 200 Mbps PyroWave stream to a Retroid Pocket 6 on 5 GHz Wi-Fi (288 Mbit/s link) lost about a quarter of its packets and 706 frames in 55 s, and its bitrate never moved. Now the same controller cuts PyroWave's bitrate on that loss, and its packet pace with it, then probes back up. The stream card says "Bitrate limited by the network". Links that carry the stream see no change.
