# Nucleus Co-op follow-up (#56)
- **Background streams survive a game's start (Experimental).** A game that swaps its splash screen for its main window, or resizes its window, while the stream opens no longer ends the first stream: the capture tries again on the game's current window for a few seconds. When the capture helper ends during start, the stream now says why instead of "Access is denied" or "The handle is invalid".
- **Unreal games fill the phone's screen (Experimental).** A game Rubylight adds to Nucleus Co-op itself starts in a window at the stream's resolution (`-ResX`/`-ResY` with `-ForceRes`, such as 2400x1080 on a 20:9 phone) instead of at its saved 16:9 resolution, even when that is larger than the PC's monitor, unless its arguments choose one.
- **No title bar in the stream (Experimental).** A game window with a title bar or sizing border (Split Fiction) loses it, so the stream shows the game alone at the stream's size.
- **The PC's keyboard and mouse stay out of DirectInput games (Experimental).** For a game whose .exe imports DirectInput, Rubylight's Nucleus Co-op handler now also keeps the PC's raw input from the game, so NieR:Automata no longer reacts to keys pressed on the PC. Other games keep their raw input, so a pad they read through it still works.
