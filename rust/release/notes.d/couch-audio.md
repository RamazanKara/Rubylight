- **Night mode** (`audio_night_mode`, off by default). A compressor, a slow leveller and a soft clipper even out loud explosions and quiet dialogue, so a stream can play quietly late at night. It adds no buffering or lookahead, keeps the balance between channels, and turns on and off without a click. NaN or infinite samples from the PC become silence and raise one `audio_bad_samples` warning.
- **Headphone surround** (`audio_headphone_surround`, off by default). A stereo client streaming System audio gets the game's 7.1 or 5.1 mix rendered for headphones with SADIE II KU100 HRIRs (Apache-2.0), at the same loudness as today's stereo downmix. The wire stays stereo Opus, so every Moonlight client works. The host captures 8 channels only when it is on at stream start; with stereo speakers on the PC it stays off and says why (`stereo_endpoint`).
- Both can be set for the host, per device and per app, or for one session with the launch parameters `audioNightMode` and `audioHeadphoneSurround`.
