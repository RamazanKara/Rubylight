# Webhooks and QR pairing
- **Webhooks.** The console's new Webhooks page (Settings, Network) sends signed JSON events to your URLs when devices pair or are removed, apps start and stop, and streams begin and end. Each hook has its own secret, shown once, and a Send test button; deliveries are retried, never hold up a stream, and each hook shows its last result. See docs/webhooks.md for verifying signatures and the 5-minute replay window.
- Experimental: **Pair with a QR code.** With Pair with a QR code on in Settings, the Devices page shows a QR code that Nightjar scans to pair without typing a PIN. It holds a one-time PIN that works once and expires after 3 minutes, and Nightjar checks the PC's certificate against it.
