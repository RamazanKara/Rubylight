## en
- Settings share one layout on phones, TVs and computers: the same eight sections, names and order on Android and desktop, with rarely changed rows folded under More.
- Sharper colored text is one choice, Off or Automatic: Nightjar picks the sharpest way the PC and this device both support, keeps HDR, and says whether the PC or the device is missing it.
- Controllers (Off, One player, A player each) and When Nightjar opens (Home, Show my PCs, Continue my last game, A chosen game) are one choice each. Your current settings carry over.
- A setting that depends on another appears only while that one is on, and a setting that can't work on this device or PC isn't offered.
- Tapping Play or Resume on Home works on the first tap after using a controller.
- All games on TVs and handhelds has Home's PC switcher and search.
- The PIN pad and the pairing code show on their own background.
- The stream menu keeps Disconnect and Quit game at the bottom; End session moved under More, and its question says what it does.
- One set of words everywhere: Wake up, PC instead of host, and Online, Offline or Not paired.

## desktop
- Starting a game fills the window with its cover while the stream connects. If the start fails, Try again, Stream settings and Test the network are right there.
- Theme: System, Dark or OLED black.
- Presets, About with every credit, Check for updates now, and Report a problem, which saves a report with personal details removed.
- Microphone noise suppression (on by default) and automatic gain.
- The connection test shows ping, jitter and loss next to the speed.
- Latency benchmark, Export latency CSV, average decode time after a stream, Keep only the newest frame and small box art, as on Android.
- PC cards show the game they run or last ran, libraries load with placeholder covers, and the top bar stays readable while a page scrolls.
