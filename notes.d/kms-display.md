## desktop
- Linux: Nightjar runs without a desktop session. **Settings › Advanced › Display output** (Automatic, Desktop, Straight to the display): Automatic draws straight to the display through DRM/KMS when there is no Wayland or X11 session, such as on a text console, with input from evdev, and the whole app and command-line starts work there. `--kms` or `--no-kms` choose for one start.
- Drawing straight to the display, a stream's pictures go onto a display plane without a copy, with HDR10, variable refresh rate, content type Game and tearing flips with V-Sync off; the overlay and stream menu go on a plane above (Video planes). Upscaling, Frame Warp and other features that need the GPU renderer turn off for a stream, with a notice, when that renderer cannot be made there.
