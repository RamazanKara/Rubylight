## desktop
- On Windows, a USB device another driver holds (a controller, a fightstick, a flash drive) can be forwarded with usbipd-win instead of Zadig: choose Forward with usbipd-win in the stream controls. Windows keeps its own driver, asks for administrator rights to share the device and again to stop, and Nightjar only ever unshares what it shared itself, also at its next start after a crash.
