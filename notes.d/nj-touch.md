## en
- The on-screen controller has a Touchpad button (Settings, On-screen controls, Show Touchpad button). It clicks the touchpad when the PC's virtual controller is a DualShock 4 or DualSense; in the modern style it sits below Home and can be moved or hidden in the layout editor.
- New setting Finger taps in Multi-touch: three- and four-finger taps run their actions in Multi-touch mode too. Off by default, so games that use three or four fingers still get them.
