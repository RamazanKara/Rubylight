## desktop
- Linux: Nightjar picks the decoder for your GPU and driver. On NVIDIA with nvidia-vaapi-driver installed, it now decodes with Vulkan video, then NVDEC, instead of going through the VA-API wrapper (`NIGHTJAR_FORCE_VAAPI=1` brings the wrapper back). The log names the VA driver and the profiles it decodes, the Vulkan device and driver, the decoder order and why, and each video format with the decoder that handles it. The Flatpak ignores a host's `LIBVA_DRIVER_NAME` and `LIBVA_DRIVERS_PATH`, which could leave it without a VA driver.
- Linux: when hardware decoding is missing a piece, the stream's start says what to install: Fedora's Mesa without H.264 and HEVC (mesa-va-drivers-freeworld or the Flatpak), a Flatpak without the NVIDIA driver extension for your driver, an NVIDIA driver without Vulkan video, or Intel's legacy i965 VA driver. The log names the system, the path each picture takes to the screen and the present mode, and the time each decoder stage takes over the stream.
- Linux: Full chroma is offered only for the codecs whose decoder opens its packed picture, twice the stream's height (3840x4320 for 4K). When none does, the stream starts in 4:2:0 and says so.
