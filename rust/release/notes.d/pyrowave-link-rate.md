# PyroWave on a slow Wi-Fi link
- **PyroWave's bitrate reacts to a link that cannot carry it.** The host already lowered the bitrate of H.264, HEVC and AV1 streams when the network lost more than error correction could repair; PyroWave streams were left out. A 200 Mbps PyroWave stream to a Retroid Pocket 6 on 5 GHz Wi-Fi (288 Mbit/s link) lost about a quarter of its packets and 706 frames in 55 s, and its bitrate never moved. Now the same controller cuts PyroWave's bitrate on lost pictures, then probes back up, and the stream card says "Bitrate limited by the network". Only a lost coarse block counts as a lost picture, so occasional detail loss changes nothing. The cuts stop at PyroWave's severe-loss floor (187 Mbps at 1080p60); below it the warning says to use HEVC or AV1 for that device. PyroWave streams above 500 Mbps keep their own ceiling, up to 2 Gbps.
