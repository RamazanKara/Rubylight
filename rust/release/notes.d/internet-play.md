# Play over the internet
- **Play over the internet without port forwarding.** New setting **Play over the internet** (Settings → Network, off by default): Nightjar devices reach this PC from outside your network over a direct path both sides punch through their routers, also behind carrier-grade NAT. The PC and each paired device find each other on the public BitTorrent DHT with records encrypted with keys only they hold; no server of ours is involved. A device learns the way once while it is on the PC's network. [Play over the internet](https://github.com/RamazanKara/Rubylight/blob/main/docs/internet-play.md)
- **Automatic port forwarding with more routers.** The `upnp` setting, now **Automatic port forwarding** (off by default), also asks routers through PCP and NAT-PMP, opens the streaming ports in the router's IPv6 firewall through UPnP or PCP, renews the mappings and removes them when the host stops. Stock Moonlight reaches a forwarded port at the PC's public address, which serverinfo now reports as `ExternalIP`.
- **Internet status** in Settings → Network shows what the router mapped, each port it refused and why, the public address, the router type, and a note when the internet provider shares one address among customers (carrier-grade NAT).
