# RTX HDR and RTX Video enhancement
- **RTX HDR and RTX Video enhancement on GeForce RTX.** Where a release lists it, a separate NVIDIA download, `rubylight-nvidia-setup-<version>.exe`, adds two image enhancements that run on the PC's graphics card before encoding; the regular installer does not include them. RTX HDR turns an SDR game into HDR for a device that streams in HDR, while the PC's display stays in SDR. RTX Video enhancement cleans up and sharpens the picture at the stream's own size, with a strength from 0 to 100. Both are off until an app or the host turns them on; each app has its own switches and values on its page in **Games**, and Nightjar can turn either on or off for one PC. The NVIDIA download is not code-signed, and it updates only to the next NVIDIA build.
