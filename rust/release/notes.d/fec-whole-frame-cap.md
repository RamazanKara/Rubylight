# Recovery keyframes keep their FEC
- After a scene cut is lost, the recovery keyframe now stays small enough to keep its full FEC within Moonlight's four blocks, so random packet loss on a fast wired route no longer loses it, up to about 280 Mbps at 60 fps (where two frames still fit that FEC). In our 250 Mbps HEVC 1440p60 test with 0.3% loss, freezes over 100 ms went from 4 to 0, the slow pace and bitrate cuts no longer set in, and picture age dropped by 3 ms at p50 and p99.
