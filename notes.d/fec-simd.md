## en
- Lost video packets are rebuilt with the processor's vector instructions, 12 to 17 times faster on a desktop PC, so on lossy Wi-Fi the frames behind them wait less. With 5 % of packets lost (HEVC 1080p60, 150 Mbps), a frame's median time from arrival to the screen fell from 3.66 to 3.39 ms and the desktop client used 17 % less CPU.
