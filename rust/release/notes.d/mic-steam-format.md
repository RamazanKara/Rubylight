# Microphone format
- **The client microphone sounds like itself again.** Steam Streaming Microphone passes what the host plays straight to its recording side without converting, and with its default formats (stereo 48 kHz playing, mono 44.1 kHz recording) a voice reached games and chat apps pitched down to less than half and chopped up. The host now gives the playing side the recording side's format before it plays. Measured on the RX 7900 XT through the real devices: speech PESQ-WB 1.09 → 4.64, tones from 440 Hz to 10 kHz arrive at their own pitch and level (THD+N -66 to -74 dB). If the format can't be set, the session shows a warning instead of sounding wrong quietly.
