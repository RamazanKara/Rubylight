## Projects Rubylight builds on

- [Vibepollo](https://github.com/Nonary/Vibepollo) by [Nonary](https://github.com/Nonary), which Rubylight began as a fork of, including its native AMF encoder
- [Apollo](https://github.com/ClassicOldSong/Apollo) by [ClassicOldSong](https://github.com/ClassicOldSong)
- [Sunshine](https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine) by [LizardByte](https://github.com/LizardByte) and its contributors
- [Foundation Sunshine](https://github.com/AlkaidLab/foundation-sunshine) by [AlkaidLab](https://github.com/AlkaidLab), [qiin2333](https://github.com/qiin2333) and contributors, for the AMD encoder's low-latency defaults, statistical video FEC, dynamic HDR metadata, HDR luminance statistics and the HLG curve
- [Moonlight V+](https://github.com/qiin2333/moonlight-vplus) by [qiin2333](https://github.com/qiin2333), for the clipboard sync frame
- [PyroWave](https://github.com/Themaister/pyrowave) and [Granite](https://github.com/Themaister/Granite) by [Themaister](https://github.com/Themaister), with the PyroWave Moonlight protocol and clients by [joemossjr16](https://github.com/joemossjr16), and [volk](https://github.com/zeux/volk) by [zeux](https://github.com/zeux)
- [dovi_tool](https://github.com/quietvoid/dovi_tool) by [quietvoid](https://github.com/quietvoid), the reference for the Dolby Vision RPU writer
- [InputPlumber](https://github.com/ShadowBlip/InputPlumber) by [ShadowBlip](https://github.com/ShadowBlip), for the virtual Steam Deck controller
- [usbip-win2](https://github.com/vadimgrn/usbip-win2) by [vadimgrn](https://github.com/vadimgrn)
- [libvirtualgamepad](https://github.com/Nonary/libvirtualgamepad) by [Nonary](https://github.com/Nonary) and Chase Payne, the virtual gamepad driver, with jlobue10's Steam Controller profile, and HIDMaestro's composite DualSense descriptors by the HIDMaestro contributors
- [punktfunk](https://github.com/unom/punktfunk) by Enrico Bühler ([unom](https://github.com/unom)), whose pf-vdisplay driver Rubylight's virtual display driver is based on
- The [SADIE II](https://zenodo.org/records/10886409) head-related impulse responses by the University of York's AudioLab, for headphone surround
- [rusty_enet](https://github.com/jabuwu/rusty_enet) by [jabuwu](https://github.com/jabuwu) and [ENet](https://github.com/lsalzman/enet) by [lsalzman](https://github.com/lsalzman)
- [Annex](https://github.com/mattxslv/annex) by [mattxslv](https://github.com/mattxslv)
- [Nucleus Co-op](https://github.com/SplitScreen-Me/splitscreenme-nucleus) by [SplitScreen-Me](https://github.com/SplitScreen-Me)
- [AMDNR](https://github.com/3zwr1/AMD-NR---OptiScaler) by [3zwr1](https://github.com/3zwr1)
- [Spleen](https://github.com/fcambus/spleen) by [fcambus](https://github.com/fcambus)

[NOTICE](NOTICE) and [rust/THIRD_PARTY.md](rust/THIRD_PARTY.md) list every component and its license.
