# Image clipboard
- **Copy images (Experimental).** Devices with clipboard permission can copy images to and from the PC, besides text, as PNG. A copied bitmap or PNG on the PC goes to the device as PNG; an image from the device lands as a bitmap with alpha and as a PNG, so Windows programs paste either. Images are checked before they are decoded: at most 16384 pixels a side and 32 megapixels, and no larger than `clipboard_image_max_mb` (16 MiB by default, up to 64) in either direction. A malformed or oversized image is refused with an error, never half pasted. `clipboard_image` (on by default) turns it off. Stock Moonlight clients are unaffected.
