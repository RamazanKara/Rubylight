# Congestion control
- **Adapt to network congestion** (All settings → Picture → Bitrate and network, off by default). With a client that reports when each video packet arrived, such as Nightjar 5.2.0, the host watches for a queue building on the network: it lowers the bitrate to just under what arrived, paces video at the network's rate and raises the bitrate again when the delay settles. It works together with **Lower the bitrate when the link cannot carry it**; the lower bitrate wins. The host log shows each change, and the `stream timings` line and the end of the stream report the queueing delay and the cuts.
