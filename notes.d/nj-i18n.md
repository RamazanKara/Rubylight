## en
- Android: Co-op and Files are translated into every language Nightjar offers.
- Desktop: menus in Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), following the system language or the Language setting.
- Desktop: Japanese, Korean and Chinese text no longer shows as boxes, and Cyrillic, Polish, Turkish and Vietnamese text is in one typeface: Nightjar uses the system's font for them (Yu Gothic UI, Malgun Gothic, Microsoft YaHei UI, Microsoft JhengHei UI or Segoe UI on Windows; Noto Sans CJK and Noto Sans or DejaVu Sans on Linux).
