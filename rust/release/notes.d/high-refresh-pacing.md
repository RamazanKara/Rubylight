# High refresh rate pacing
- **Pacing that follows the frame rate above 120 fps (#144).** At 240 and 480 Hz the PC now sizes its video send batches, and the packet groups of Adaptive bitrate's **On, and react to network delay**, to the stream's frame period, so it no longer treats two or more frames as one burst. A batch holds at most a quarter of a frame (unchanged at 144 and 240 Hz, about half a millisecond at 480 Hz), and a packet group at most half a frame. Streams at 120 fps and below send exactly as before.
- **Send gaps in the log.** Every five seconds the host log adds a `video send gaps` line beside the stream timings: how many frames left the PC on time, bunched together, late or after a skipped frame period, with the mean deviation and the longest gap. It shows whether a stutter starts on the PC or on the network.
