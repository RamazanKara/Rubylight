## en
- New setting **Play over the internet** (Settings → Streaming → Connection, on by default): away from home, Nightjar reaches a PC that offers it without port forwarding. It tries your network first, then the PC's IPv6 address, then a forwarded port, then a direct path through both routers. Connect once on the PC's network first.
- The PC's line names the internet path in use; when none works, it says which paths were tried and why each failed.
