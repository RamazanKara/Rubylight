## en
- Dual screen mode: on a device with a second screen, a second stream of the same game runs on it, at that screen's own size or a fixed resolution. Sound, controllers and the microphone stay with the first screen. Needs a Rubylight PC that supports it. Thanks to @umbertomarotta (nightjar#82).

## desktop
- Dual screen mode on the desktop: with a second monitor, the second stream opens full screen on the other monitor.
