# Nucleus Co-op
- **Close Nucleus Co-op** sits next to **Open Nucleus Co-op on this PC**, and a co-op or background stream closes the Nucleus Co-op the console opened before it starts; one that won't close (it is asking something) refuses the stream until you answer it at the PC. A Nucleus Co-op you started yourself is never closed: Open and launches say so instead.
- A background stream gets a card on Home with **End stream**, and for a minute after a session ends, Home says which ended and why.
- When the Windows account signed in on the PC is a standard user, **Set up co-op**, Open and launches say that Nucleus Co-op needs an administrator account. Before, Nucleus Co-op started without administrator rights and got stuck on its loading screen with "Microsoft Edge can't read and write to its data directory".
