## en
- When the PC starts a stream after a setup fallback, such as showing its physical display because its virtual display is unavailable, Nightjar shows the PC's notice once the stream runs. An automatic reconnect does not repeat it.
- With a PC that reports it, the performance overlay shows the PC's video encoder load.

## desktop
- A PC that reports its frame limiter shows on its details page whether the limiter is on, whether it is on for the virtual display, and its frame limit.
