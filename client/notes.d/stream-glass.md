## en
- The statistics overlay on a TV stays one column along the left edge: it tightens its gaps and its pacing graph first, then uses smaller text, and only takes a second column when even that cannot hold it. It no longer runs off the bottom of the screen.
- The stream menu is easier to read over a bright or HDR game, above all on devices without live blur such as the Shield TV; devices that blur keep the glass look.
- Dialogs over a stream, both on-screen keyboards, the statistics and the controller editor now wear the same glass as the stream menu. Keys are solid caps, so their labels read over any game, and a keyboard's opacity setting now fades only its panel. Nothing blurs behind what stays up while you play.

## desktop
- The statistics and stream notices wear the stream menu's glass, without any blur behind them.
