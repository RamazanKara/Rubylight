# Guests from other devices
- **Play together from your own devices.** Turn it on with **When another device joins the running game** (Settings → Games → Play together): with **Ask the player** or **Let in without asking**, a second paired device that joins the game another device plays becomes its guest: the same picture and sound with its own stream, its own controller as the next player, and the keyboard and mouse only if you grant them. Nightjar's Join and a Moonlight client starting the running app both work.
- **You decide who joins.** With **Ask the player** the player lets each guest in, from Nightjar or the console's Home, within 45 seconds. Off by default, so your own devices still join or resume the running game without asking. **What a guest may do** sets View only, Controller, or Controller, keyboard and mouse, and the player can change it, kick a guest, block it for the rest of the game, or let it in for an hour or a day.
- **One keyboard and mouse at a time.** A guest with keyboard and mouse takes them when nobody else used them for two seconds; the player, or someone at the PC moving its mouse, takes them back at once.
- The player's own stream is unchanged: each guest gets its own encoder on the same capture, at its own size, codec and bitrate.
