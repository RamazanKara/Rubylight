## en
- With "Full chroma" on, a stream no longer hangs while starting: when the PC or this device can't do full chroma, the stream starts without it and says so.
- Stopping a stream that does not end within 10 seconds now shows an error instead of waiting.
- Y (and F on a keyboard) on Home's game cards and lists now opens the game's options instead of starting the game.
- Dialogs and sheets outside the stream blur the page behind them on Android 12 and newer, where the system offers blur.
- Settings → Streaming has a new "Network tests" entry that opens a saved PC's connection test and frame pacing calibration.
