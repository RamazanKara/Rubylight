# DualShock 4 on ViGEmBus
- **DualShock 4 (ViGEmBus) controller type, for reWASD and other tools that look for USB controllers.** With ViGEmBus installed, every controller becomes a USB DualShock 4 that such tools detect and remap, and unlike Xbox 360 (ViGEmBus) it keeps the touchpad, gyro, battery, rumble and lightbar. A lightbar colour set by a game or tool reaches the controller in your hand where the client can light it, and Back also clicks the touchpad unless you turn off Back clicks the touchpad. Without ViGEmBus the stream says what to install; no other controller stands in. (#108)
