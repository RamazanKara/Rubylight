## en
- Full chroma: a PC that refused it is asked again after Nightjar or the PC is updated, not only when the setting is switched.
- When a PC refuses full chroma after the game has started, the stream starts again right away without it and resumes the game.
- A start that waits for a previous connection to close, or for a PC that never answers the launch, now ends with an error instead of waiting forever. Cancel still works while it waits.
## desktop
- Full chroma: a PC that refused it is asked again after Nightjar or the PC is updated.
- A PC that never answers the launch ends the start after 2 minutes with an error.
