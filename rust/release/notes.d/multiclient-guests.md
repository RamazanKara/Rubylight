# Guests from other devices
- **Play together from your own devices.** Turn it on with **When another device joins the running game** (Settings → Games → Play together, off by default): with **Ask the player** or **Let in without asking**, a second paired device that joins the game another device plays becomes its guest, with the same picture and sound on its own stream. Nightjar's Join and a Moonlight client starting the running app both work. While it is off, your own devices join or resume the running game without asking, as before.
- **You decide who joins.** With **Ask the player** the player lets each guest in, from Nightjar or the console's Home, within 45 seconds. **What a guest may do** sets View only, Controller (the default: its own controller as the next player) or Controller, keyboard and mouse. A view-only guest gets no controller, keyboard, mouse or clipboard on the PC. The player can change a guest's permission during the game, kick it, block it for the rest of the game, or let it in for an hour or a day.
- **One keyboard and mouse at a time.** A guest with keyboard and mouse takes them when nobody else used them for two seconds; the player, or someone at the PC using its own mouse or keyboard, takes them back at once.
- The player's own stream is unchanged: each guest gets its own encoder on the same capture, at its own size, codec and bitrate.
