# Fixed-rate pacing
- **Steadier fixed-rate streams beside other windows.** When a game runs at two to four times the stream rate (120 Hz at 60 fps), the host now follows the game's own frame timing, so another window's updates and game frames that miss a refresh no longer skip the game's frames or delay the frames after them. Measured on the RX 7900 XT at 1080p60, with a 120 Hz game and another window animating at 30 or 90 fps: end to end p95 was 1.5 to 2.2 ms lower and picture age p95 3.0 to 5.5 ms lower. Before the change, up to 17 % of frames sent repeated or skipped a game frame; after it, at most 0.3 %.
