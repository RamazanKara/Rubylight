- **Wake-on-LAN and sleep in the console.** Help & diagnostics has a new Power section. It shows whether devices can wake this PC, with every physical adapter and its wake settings, as well as Sleep, Fast Startup and the sleep-after-idle countdown. **Apply changes** turns on exactly the listed adapter settings after you confirm, and Fast Startup can be turned off at the same time. If the adapters changed since the last check, the change is refused and the new list is shown.
- **Sleep after idle in Settings.** General > Power sets `sleep_after_idle_minutes` (0, or 5 to 1440) and, once it is on, `sleep_while_suspended`.
