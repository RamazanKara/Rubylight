Stream your games from any AMD Radeon PC to any screen: a laptop, TV, phone, tablet, handheld or another PC. Rubylight drives AMD's encoder natively, prepares every frame on Radeon compute, and carries HEVC and AV1 in 10-bit HDR, or full-colour HDR 4:4:4 through PyroWave. It works with Moonlight and every other Sunshine-compatible client.

All performance results and what each number means

What you get

The Radeon frame path, end to end

Radeon compute

Frame copies and colour conversion run on D3D12 compute queues beside the game's graphics work, instead of waiting behind it.

Native AMF encoding

AMD's encoder driven directly, not through a generic wrapper, and tuned per codec from VMAF measurements. Driver quirks get workarounds, and lose them once AMD fixes the driver.

HEVC and AV1 HDR

Ten-bit BT.2020/PQ from capture to the encoder, with HDR metadata in the keyframes, checked by a test. H.264 is there too.

PyroWave HDR 4:4:4

A colour sample for every pixel keeps coloured text and edges crisp, over a fast local network with Nightjar, an Android client with PyroWave support, or any other PyroWave-capable Moonlight client.

Virtual displays

A display per device, shared or off, with exclusive, extended, primary and isolated layouts, HDR, and your own layout restored after the stream.

Your library

Steam and Playnite sync, per-app and per-device settings, RTSS frame limits, Lossless Scaling, and a 1000 Hz VRR mode.

Controllers

Xbox, DualSense with adaptive triggers and Switch Pro pads from one signed driver. With usbip-win2 installed, a Steam Deck can show up on the host as a real Steam Deck controller.

Rust throughout

Streaming, protocol, native helpers, the Windows service and the installer are Rust, with a web console for pairing, the library and live encoder timings.

Install. Pair Moonlight. Play.

Three steps to your first stream

  1. Install

    Run the installer from the release. Installing over Apollo, Vibepollo or Butterpollo keeps your settings, paired devices, app library and covers.

  2. Open the console

    Go to https://localhost:47990 on the host PC and create your local account.

  3. Pair and play

    Add your PC in Moonlight or any Sunshine-compatible client, enter its PIN under Devices, then launch Desktop. Start at 1080p60, then raise resolution, frame rate and HDR.

Why it exists

AMD rarely gets any love in this space

The protocol started as NVIDIA's GameStream, and the hosts that replaced it kept NVIDIA first. Upstream Sunshine has had a native NVENC encoder since 2023, while AMD still goes through FFmpeg's generic AMF wrapper. So Radeon owners have spent years hearing their cards are just worse at streaming. Most of the time the card was never the problem. Nobody had sat down with it.

Rubylight is a host rebuilt in Rust around the Radeon frame path, from capture to the encoder, for every Radeon owner who wants to play away from the desk.
