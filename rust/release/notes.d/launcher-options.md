# Launchers
- **Every launcher works like Steam.** Epic Games, Battle.net, Microsoft Store and Xbox, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, GOG and your game folders each have their own options under All settings › Games: sync automatically, remove games you uninstall, and games to leave out. Battle.net can also keep only the games you played recently, as Steam can. Your earlier shared choices carry over to every launcher.
- Turning off a launcher's automatic sync now stops only the background check; Sync now still adds its games, as with Steam.
- Epic games get their box art from the Epic Games launcher's own files. A cover you chose is never replaced.
- A synced game's page says which launcher starts it, saving it confirms with a short message instead of a banner, and a launcher that isn't installed shows a switch you can't turn on.
