## en
- On the Xiaomi FX Mini LED Fire TV (AFTNE4E43F), Android's low-latency decoding now starts off once, and again after Reset all settings, because the picture could break into coloured blocks with it on. Decoding takes longer with it off. If you turn it back on, Nightjar keeps that choice and works to limit the blocks: it asks the PC for a single reference frame, asks for a fresh keyframe when decode time climbs and when the climb ends, on a calm picture after 10 seconds and at least every 30 seconds, and spaces out frames that arrive right behind each other. Other devices don't change.
- The performance overlay says only "hot" or "warm" unless Thermal protection is actually adjusting the stream.
- Problem reports name the device, put a UTC time on each line and list the last 8 streams, and slow decoder input makes one summary line per 10 seconds instead of filling the log. They also say why Nightjar changed the bitrate.
