- **Control messages arrive right after a network outage.** When the network came back after about 10 s, what the host had sent in the meantime (an end of session, rumble, HDR metadata changes, link notices) could take another 1 to 3 s to reach the device, and in a simulation the host sometimes dropped a device that had been back for seconds. The host now resends these messages as soon as it hears from the device again: about 85 ms after a 10 s outage on loopback, against 1.0 to 2.9 s before. Every Moonlight client benefits with no client changes; in steady 5 % loss the host resends exactly as before.
