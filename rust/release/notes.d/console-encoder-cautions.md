# Encoder settings that cost the stream
- **The console says when an encoder setting slows the stream.** An AMD setting changed to a value that adds delay or weakens recovery now says what it costs under its menu, and the Settings page lists every such setting you changed, with a link to each. This covers the usages that queue frames at 4K (Driver default means AMD's transcoding profile), the Quality preset (about 1 ms more per 4K frame on HEVC and 12 ms on AV1 than Speed), pre-analysis (about 20 ms per 4K frame on 8-bit SDR streams), the low-latency modes turned off, and Maximum frame size on Driver default, where a large keyframe can outgrow error correction. The numbers are our RX 7900 XT measurements. Dismiss the list once and it stays hidden until another costly value appears.
