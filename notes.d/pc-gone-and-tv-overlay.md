## en
- A PC that shuts down during a stream now ends it within seconds with a message, instead of a frozen picture for 10 to 20 seconds. A host that crashes or restarts still reconnects on its own, and Wi-Fi dropouts and a stalled PC still ride it out.
- Ending a stream from a PC that went silent no longer waits two seconds for an answer that won't come.
- On Android TV the full statistics overlay now fits on screen.
