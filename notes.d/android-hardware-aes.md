## en
- Encrypted video, the default for streams over the internet, uses the phone's AES instructions instead of software on the new streaming engine. Decryption is about four times faster on a Retroid Pocket 6: at 80 Mbps it takes 1.6 % of a CPU core, down from 6.2 %, and a live 40 Mbps stream's receive thread went from 8.4 % to 5.5 %.
