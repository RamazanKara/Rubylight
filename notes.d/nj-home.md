## en
- Home on Android no longer says "No PCs yet" for a few seconds after a start while your paired PCs are still answering; it shows that it is loading instead.
- A paired PC that is off shows on Home as an offline card with Wake, also before it has shared its games once, and a PC that does not answer within 10 seconds shows as offline instead of Home loading on.
- L1 and R1 switch between Home, PCs and Settings (and Help on Windows and Linux) with a controller, round from the last tab to the first.
- Home has a third layout under View: List shows a line per game with a small cover, its store and when you last played it, and works with touch, a D-pad and a controller.
