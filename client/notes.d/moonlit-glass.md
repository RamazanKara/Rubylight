## en
- A new look: moonlit glass on phones, handhelds, TVs and desktop. Home, PCs, the game grid, game pages, Settings, dialogs, the PIN pad, pairing, connecting and the stream menu share one design, with System, Dark and OLED themes on Android and a plain look for high contrast.
- The glass fits the device: a live blur where the GPU keeps up, a still one where it doesn't. Nothing glass is drawn over a stream unless the menu is open, and a stream on a Radeon 780M laptop measured the same as before.
- Button hints follow what you used last: remote, controller (with PlayStation labels on Sony pads) or keyboard, and none for touch.
- Play on Home asks before it closes a game that is already running, as the game page does.
- Confirmations that end a game, a session or a pairing start on Cancel.
- Turning, folding or splitting the screen rebuilds the layout for the new shape.
- The stream menu says Quit game, as the rest of the app does.

## desktop
- With two or more PCs, the PC button at the top opens a list of them; with one it opens that PC's options.
- On a controller, Y opens a game's options, as on Android, and removing a favourite is in that menu. The keyboard keeps Delete.
