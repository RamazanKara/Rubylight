## en
- VRR on a TV whose top refresh rate is only offered at a smaller resolution (1080p at 120 Hz beside 4K at 60 Hz): a 4K stream now stays at 4K and asks the PC for the 60 fps the screen shows, instead of 120 fps that the TV box either dropped to 1080p for or showed only half of. Streams the smaller mode holds whole, such as 1080p, still get 120 Hz.
