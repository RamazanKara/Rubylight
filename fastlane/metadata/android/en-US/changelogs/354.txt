10-bit color (SDR): a new Stream setting that streams SDR in 10-bit HEVC or AV1 to reduce banding on screens without HDR. It needs a Main10 decoder on this device; if the PC does not offer 10-bit, the stream is 8-bit and Nightjar says so. Experimental: on Android TV with HDR, a stream now ends in steps (Shield and similar), and a TV that drops the signal no longer triggers a false decoder-crash dialog.
