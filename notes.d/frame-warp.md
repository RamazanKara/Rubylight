## en
- Frame Warp (Video, off by default): with a Rubylight PC, a mouse or right-stick camera turn shows on the next refresh of your screen. Each picture is shifted by the turn the PC has not drawn yet, and the game's HUD stays in place. Needs the New streaming engine.

## desktop
- Frame Warp on Windows, Linux and macOS, with every presenter; a 60 fps stream turns at the screen's own rate on a faster screen.
