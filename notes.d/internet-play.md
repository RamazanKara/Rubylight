## en
- New setting **Play over the internet** (Settings → Streaming → Connection, on by default): away from home, Nightjar reaches a PC without port forwarding when that PC has Play over the internet turned on (it is off by default on the PC). It tries your network first, then the PC's IPv6 address, then a forwarded port, then a direct path through both routers. Connect once on the PC's network first.
- The PC's line names the internet path in use; when none works, it says which paths were tried and why each failed.
