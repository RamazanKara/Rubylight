# AMD conversion and capture
- **Hardware color conversion.** A new AMD AMF setting, **Hardware color conversion** (off by default), converts and scales YUV 4:2:0 frames in SDR, HDR10 and HLG with the driver's video converter instead of Rubylight's shaders, and hands the result to the encoder without a copy. Frames it does not cover, such as full-chroma streams, the desktop pointer drawn by Rubylight, the couch menu and adjusted HDR brightness, keep the shaders, and a converter failure returns the stream to the shaders with a console warning. The log reports conversion and capture-to-submit times every 10 seconds.
- **AMD Direct Capture.** A new capture method, **AMD Direct Capture**, takes each picture the Radeon driver presents on a Radeon-connected display, up to the display's refresh rate. Automatic does not select it; without a supported Radeon driver it falls back to Windows Graphics Capture with a console warning. Frame generation's extra frames, such as AMD Fluid Motion Frames, are not streamed.
