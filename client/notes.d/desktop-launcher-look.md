## en
- Desktop: a new launcher look. Cards lighten under the pointer instead of drawing outlines, the focus is one amber ring, and the top bar shows where you are.
- Desktop: Home shows a row for each store (Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Battle.net, Xbox and more). A row with more games than fit ends in an All tile that opens All games, where chips filter by store or by what is installed.
- Desktop: a PC's or game's options open as a menu right by its card, from the three dots, a right-click, F2 or the controller's X, with icons and shortcuts.
