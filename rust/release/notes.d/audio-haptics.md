- **Haptics from game audio** (`audio_haptics`, off by default). A client DualSense with waveform haptics feels the game's bass and impacts while the game sends it no vibration: the bass below 150 Hz drives each actuator from its side (the LFE drives both), and sharp transients add a short thump. **Audio haptics strength** (`audio_haptics_strength`, 0 to 200 %), **response** (`audio_haptics_curve`: linear, soft, punchy) and **noise gate** (`audio_haptics_gate`, -80 to -10 dBFS) apply mid-stream when saved globally or per app, and per device from the next stream. It stops the moment the game sends its own haptics, rumble or trigger rumble, and resumes a minute after the last. The audio path only copies sent packets into a lock-free ring while a controller wants them, so audio gains no latency; no USB/IP is needed.
