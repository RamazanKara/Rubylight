# Guests in the browser
- **Share your browser stream with friends (#67).** While you stream on the console's Stream page, **Guests → Invite** makes a link for up to four guests: view only, controller, or controller with keyboard and mouse, lasting 1 hour, 24 hours or a time you pick (5 minutes to 7 days). Guests open it in their browser, type a name and see and hear your stream; no app, account or pairing. The list shows who watches, with Kick, and every link, with Revoke. Restarting Rubylight revokes every link.
- **One encode, no relay.** The PC encodes once and each guest gets the same frames over its own connection, so a guest on a slow link drops frames alone instead of holding up yours. Guests connect straight to the PC: nothing to set up on the local network; from the internet forward TCP 47990 and UDP 8002-8005, and the console itself still answers only on your local network ([Guests in the browser](https://github.com/RamazanKara/Rubylight/blob/main/docs/browser-guests.md)).
- **Safe by default.** The link's token travels only after `#` and the host keeps just its hash; wrong links are rate-limited per address; a guest's page reaches nothing of the console, and the host checks every guest input against the invite's permission. No text or voice chat.
