# Internet security
- **A warning for unencrypted internet streams.** With Internet encryption on its default, When the device supports it, a device outside your network that cannot encrypt still streams, and the console now warns that its stream crosses the internet unencrypted. Required turns such devices away, and its refusal now says how to fix it. Tailscale counts as your local network.
- **Pairing requests from the internet say so.** The tray notice and the console's pairing request say when a device asks from outside your network, and ask you to enter its PIN only if you expect it.
