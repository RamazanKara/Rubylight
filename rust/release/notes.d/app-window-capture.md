# One app's window
- **Stream one app's window instead of a display.** Set **What to stream** to **The app's window** for an app in **Games**, for one device in its **Per device** panel, or for every app under **Picture → Capture**. The host finds the window among the programs the app started, or by a program name and title you set, and keeps its shape with black bars. Resizing, switching between windowed, borderless and fullscreen, DPI changes and moving the window to another monitor are followed.
- **Input lands on the window.** Mouse, touch and pen positions map onto the streamed window, wherever it is on the PC's screen.
- **Minimise and restore.** While the window is minimised the stream shows the display and the device says why; restore it on the PC and the stream goes back to the window. A window that is still opening when the stream starts gets a moment to appear, so the stream starts on it. Before the window appears and for 5 seconds after it closes the stream shows the display; **When the window closes** can end the stream instead.
