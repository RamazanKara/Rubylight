## desktop
- The statistics overlay and the log name PyroWave's chroma ("PyroWave 4:4:4", "PyroWave 10-bit 4:4:4").
- The log at the end of a PyroWave stream gives the decoder's own GPU time, and on Linux with a Radeon GPU also the GPU clocks during the stream, so a slow decode can be told apart from a busy or slowly clocked GPU.
