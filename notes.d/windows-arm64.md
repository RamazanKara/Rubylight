## desktop
- Windows on ARM: Nightjar now comes as a native ARM64 build for Snapdragon X laptops and other ARM64 PCs (`nightjar-desktop-<version>-windows-arm64-setup.exe` and `.zip`), so it no longer runs the x86_64 build under emulation (nightjar#102). An installed x86_64 Nightjar on an ARM64 PC switches to it with its next update and keeps its settings and paired PCs.
