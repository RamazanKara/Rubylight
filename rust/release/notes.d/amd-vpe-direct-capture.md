# AMD conversion and capture
- **Hardware color conversion.** A new AMD AMF setting, **Hardware color conversion** (`amd_vpe`, off by default), has the graphics driver's video converter convert and scale YUV 4:2:0 pictures in SDR, HDR10 and HLG instead of Rubylight's shaders. On the RX 7900 XT and the Radeon 780M it gave lower picture quality than the default conversion in every case we measured, and more latency at 1440p 120 fps and in HDR, so it stays off; it remains a setting to compare on your own PC. Full-chroma streams, the pointer drawn by Rubylight, the couch menu and adjusted HDR brightness always use the shaders. A converter failure returns the stream to the shaders with a console warning, and a converter that keeps refusing pictures is left off for the rest of the stream.
- **AMD Direct Capture.** A new capture method, **AMD Direct Capture** (`capture = amd`), takes each picture the Radeon driver presents on a display connected to the Radeon card, up to the display's refresh rate (at most 200 frames per second). Choose it under Capture; Automatic never picks it. Without a supported Radeon driver the stream falls back to Windows Graphics Capture and the console says why. Frame generation's extra frames are not streamed, with this or any other capture method.
