# Clipboard sync
- **Clipboard sync.** Text copied on the device goes on the PC's clipboard, and text copied on the PC goes to the device, as you copy it, without opening a menu. It runs with Moonlight V+ clients and with Nightjar when its Clipboard sync setting is on. The device's copies need "Write to the clipboard" and the PC's "Read the clipboard" in the device's permissions; PNG images go too, for clients that carry them, while `clipboard_image` is on. A copy the permissions refuse is logged and shown on the stream card, never applied in part. Stock Moonlight clients and their DESCRIBE answers are unchanged.
