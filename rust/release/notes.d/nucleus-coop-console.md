# Nucleus Co-op
- **Close Nucleus Co-op** sits next to **Open Nucleus Co-op on this PC**, and a co-op or background stream closes the Nucleus Co-op the console opened before it starts; one that won't close (it is asking something) refuses the stream until you answer it at the PC. A Nucleus Co-op you started yourself is never closed: Open and launches say so instead.
- A background stream gets a card on Home with **End stream**, and for a minute after a session ends, Home says which ended and why.
- **Set up co-op** no longer leaves Nucleus Co-op stuck on its loading screen with "Microsoft Edge can't read and write to its data directory". WebView2 runs its browser without administrator rights even for an elevated Nucleus Co-op, so the handler hub view now keeps its data in the signed-in user's `AppData\Local\Butterpollo\nucleus-webview`.
- When Windows gives the account signed in on the PC no administrator rights to start Nucleus Co-op with (a standard account, or Windows' Administrator protection), **Set up co-op**, Open and launches that need those rights say so. Before, Nucleus Co-op started without administrator rights and got stuck on its loading screen with "Microsoft Edge can't read and write to its data directory".
