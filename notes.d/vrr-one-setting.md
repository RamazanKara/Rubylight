## en
- Variable refresh rate is one setting with three choices: Off, On and Fastest. On shows each frame at the game's timing (Real VRR); Fastest shows each frame the moment it arrives. It replaces the two switches Variable refresh rate and Real VRR, and what you chose there is kept.

## desktop
- On the desktop the one Variable refresh rate setting also replaces Tear-free VRR: On shows whole frames, Fastest can tear. A mix of the old switches that is neither shows as Custom and works as before until you choose another. settings.json keeps its three values (`vrr`, `present_at_host_times`, `vrr_tear_free`).
