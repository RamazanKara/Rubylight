# Locked PC cold launch
- **A stream starts on a locked PC whose display is off.** Starting a stream on a locked PC, or one whose display Windows had switched off, could end with "capture produced no GPU frame" after 10 seconds. Rubylight now keeps the PC's display on for as long as a stream runs (Windows otherwise switches it off a minute into the lock screen), and when a launch gets no picture within a second it wakes the display, physical or virtual, with the same tiny mouse move a device's first input makes, on the lock screen when the PC is locked, and tries again while the display powers up. A launch that shows a picture right away wakes nothing, and the display may switch off again once the stream ends.
